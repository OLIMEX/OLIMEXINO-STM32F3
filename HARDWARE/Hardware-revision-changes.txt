9. CON1 renamed to CAN1;
10. CE sign added;
12. MICRO_SD1 changed from TFC-WPAPR-08 to TFC-09-1.7H(ATFFS150A01BR016);

14. RoHS_Sign added;
15. CON3's name changed from invisible to visible;
16. R2 changed from 1.5K/0402 to 10K/0402.
